Enable beta features?

Image viewing and image creation are experimental beta features. They're not polished — they can be slow, misfire, or misunderstand what you ask for. They also call extra models through Puter, so turning this on will use more of your Puter usage allowance.

Show advanced settings?

These settings change how the AI works — the model, image model, system prompt, temperature and reply length. A wrong value (for example a model that doesn't exist) can make replies fail or behave strangely. For everyday use, the model picker under the input box is all you need.

Help

Pick a category to see what each feature does and how to use it.

Welcome! This is a quick tour. Use the categories above to learn what each feature does and how to use it.

1. Sign in to Puter

The AI runs through a free Puter account. Press Sign in to Puter on the welcome screen, or later in Settings → Connection. Use a personal Microsoft or Apple account — open the Signing in category to see why.

2. Send your first message

Click the box at the bottom that says Message…, type something and press Enter (or click the arrow button). Shift + Enter starts a new line.

3. Add your name (optional)

Type it in Settings → Behavior → Personalization so the AI can greet you by name.

Know the buttons

The three-line button (top left) shows or hides your chat list. Top right: the monitor changes the tab icon, the ? opens this help, the theme button switches light and dark, and the gear opens Settings.

Your data stays on this device

Chats and settings are stored only in this browser. AI requests go through Puter.

ImportantUse a personal Apple or personal Microsoft account to log in to Puter. Google and email sign-in are not available on the Chromebooks used here.

Why can’t I use Google or email with Puter?

Logging in to Puter with Google or with an email address isn’t available on the Chromebooks used here, so those options can’t be used to sign in. That includes your school Google account.

The only options that work are a personal Apple account or a personal Microsoft account — not a school or work account.

How to sign in

  1. Press Sign in to Puter: on the welcome screen, in Settings → Connection, or with the sign in link under the message box when you aren’t signed in.
  2. A Puter pop-up opens. Choose Microsoft or Apple.
  3. Log in with your personal account.
  4. The pop-up closes and you’re signed in. If you pressed Send first, your message is sent automatically.

I don’t have a personal Apple or Microsoft account

You’ll need to create one first (they’re free), then use it here. A free Puter account is created for you automatically the first time you sign in.

The pop-up didn’t open

Sign-in uses a pop-up window. Allow pop-ups for this page in your browser, then press the sign-in button again.

Signing out

Open Settings → Connection and press Sign out.

Skipping sign-in

You can skip it on the welcome screen, but the AI can’t reply to your messages until you sign in.

Send a message

Type in the message box and press Enter. If you prefer Ctrl/⌘ + Enter, change it in Settings → Behavior → Sending messages. Shift + Enter always adds a new line.

Stop a reply

While the AI is writing, the send button turns into a stop button. Click it or press Esc.

Edit and resend

Hover over one of your messages and click the pencil. Shortcut: press ↑ in an empty message box to pull your last message back for editing.

Regenerate a reply

Hover over the latest AI reply and click the circular arrow to get a fresh answer.

Copy a message

Hover over any message and click the copy icon.

Read aloud

Hover over an AI reply and click the speaker. Click it again to stop. Choose the voice in Settings → Connection → Read-aloud voice.

Math mode (Σ)

Click Σ in the message box to turn math mode on — it lights up. The AI then acts as a calculator, shows brief working and ends with a clear final answer. Click Σ again to turn it off.

Jump to the newest message

If you scroll up, a down-arrow button appears at the bottom of the chat. Click it to jump back down.

Attach files or photos

Click the + button next to the message box, pick one or more files, then send. You can attach images, text files (.txt, .md, .csv, .json, code), PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint.

Drag and drop

Drag a file onto the chat. Drop to attach appears — let go to add it.

Paste an image or file

Copy it, click the message box and press Ctrl/⌘ + V.

What the AI can read

Text files are included as text. Images are read by a vision model — turn on Enable beta features in Settings → Connection first. PDF, Word, Excel and similar files are attached by name only, so the AI can’t read inside them.

Remove an attachment

Use the remove button on the attachment before you send.

View an image bigger

Click an image in the chat to open it full size. Press Esc to close it.

Long pasted text

If you paste more than 200 words, it turns into a PASTED chip instead of filling the box. Click the chip to edit the text or Remove it. It is sent as part of your message.

Voice input (microphone)

Click the microphone, allow microphone access if the browser asks, and speak. Your words appear in the message box. Click the microphone again to stop. Pick the language in Settings → Connection → Voice input language.

Turn notes or any topic into practice. Just ask in the chat — no special button needed.

Get a quiz

Ask for one, for example “Quiz me on photosynthesis” or “Test me on these notes”. Paste or attach your notes first, or say “quiz me on that” to use the earlier chat. It works in many languages.

Answer a quiz

Click an answer. Use Show hint if it’s offered, read the feedback, then press Next. At the end you get a score, a list of missed questions, and buttons to retry the missed ones or start over.

Get flashcards

Ask for them, for example “Make flashcards from these notes”. Click a card to flip it.

Use flashcards

Mark each card Again or Known, or move with the arrow buttons. Shuffle and Copy are under the card. At the end you can review the cards you didn’t know.

New chat

Click New chat at the top of the sidebar or press Ctrl/⌘ + K.

Switch, show or hide chats

Click a chat in the list to open it. Show or hide the list with the three-line button (top left) or Ctrl/⌘ + B.

Search chats

Type in Search chats… or press Ctrl/⌘ + F.

Rename a chat

Double-click its title, type a new name and press Enter. You can also type /rename New name in the message box.

Pin a chat

Hover over a chat and click the pin icon to keep it at the top. Click it again to unpin.

Let the AI rename a chat

Hover over a chat and click the circular arrow to have the AI write a new title.

Delete a chat

Hover over a chat and click the delete button. To remove every chat at once, use Settings → Behavior → Delete all.

Duplicate, copy or export

Ctrl/⌘ + D (or /duplicate) makes a copy of the chat. Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + C (or /copy) copies it as markdown. /export downloads it as a file.

Drafts are kept

Text you haven’t sent yet is remembered separately for each chat.

Copy code

Every code block has a Copy button in its header.

Run HTML

Code blocks labelled html also have a blue Run button. It opens the page full screen. Close it with the × button or Esc.

Keyword hints

Inside code blocks, hover over keywords such as function or return to see a short explanation.

Type a command in the message box and press Enter. Commands are not sent to the AI.

/clearDelete all messages in the current chat (asks you to confirm first).
/rename <title>Rename the current chat.
/system <prompt>Set instructions that are sent with every message. Leave the prompt empty to clear it.
/model <name>Switch to another AI model.
/temp <0–1.5>Set the temperature. Lower is more focused, higher is more creative.
/exportDownload the current chat as a file.
/copyCopy the current chat as markdown.
/duplicateMake a copy of the current chat.
/helpShow a quick reminder of these commands.

Open Settings with the gear (top right). It has three tabs. Press Save to keep your changes.

Connection tab

Sign in or out of Puter, turn on beta features, and choose the Model, Image model and System prompt. Temperature sets focused vs creative replies and Max output tokens caps how long a reply can be. Also set the Voice input language and the Read-aloud voice.

Appearance tab

Theme (light, dark or system), accent colour, text size, compact layout, bubble style, font, message width, animations, tab icon and Reply language (make the AI always answer in one language).

Behavior tab

Your name, whether Enter or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter sends, streaming replies, a sound or desktop notification when a reply finishes, and Delete all chats (this can’t be undone).

Quick light/dark switch

The theme button in the top right toggles light and dark without opening Settings.

Image viewing and image creation are experimental. They can be slow or misunderstand you, and they use more of your Puter usage.

Turn them on

Go to Settings → Connection, switch on Enable beta features and confirm the warning.

Ask about a photo

Attach or paste an image, then ask a question about it. If needed, choose an Image model in Settings → Connection.

Create an image

Ask for a picture in any language, for example “Draw a cat in space”. It appears in the chat.

Change an image

After an image is made, say things like “make it bigger” or “add a hat” to resize or edit it.

Change the tab icon

Click the monitor icon (top right). Classroom and Udir change the browser tab’s icon and text right away. Reset icon goes back to Classroom.

Forms screen

Pick Forms to set up the “already answered” screen: type a headline, tab text and an optional hotkey, then press Open screen. Reset all clears those three.

Vigilo screen

Pick Vigilo to open the Vigilo login screen straight away.

Leave a screen

Press Esc, double-click an empty part of the background, or press your hotkey again.

Set a hotkey

In the Forms settings, click the Hotkey box and press a key combination (Esc cancels). It opens and closes the last screen you used, and works from anywhere in the app. Clear removes it.

The AI doesn’t reply

You’re most likely not signed in. Press Sign in to Puter (Settings → Connection) and use a personal Apple or Microsoft account.

Sign-in didn’t finish

Press the sign-in button and try again. Make sure pop-ups are allowed for this page, and don’t use Google or email — see the Signing in category.

The microphone doesn’t work

Allow microphone access for this page in the browser and check that a microphone is connected.

Replies stop too early

Raise Max output tokens in Settings → Connection.

The AI ignores my image

Image viewing is a beta feature. Turn on Enable beta features in Settings → Connection.

My chats are gone

Chats are stored only in this browser. They won’t appear in another browser or profile, or after the site data is cleared.
