Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

At WTFQ, we value your privacy. This page explains how information is handled when using our website and its tools.

1. General Website Usage

WTFQ provides browser-based tools, games and other resources. Some features may interact with external websites and third-party services.

External websites have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices. WTFQ is not responsible for how those websites handle your information.

2. Autofiller

WTFQ Autofiller processes uploaded DOCX documents in your browser. When you choose to use AI autofill, relevant extracted text, such as questions, field labels and document excerpts, is sent to Puter's AI service to generate suggestions.

This means that document information may be processed by a third-party AI provider. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal or confidential information.

Puter's own privacy policy and terms apply to its processing of AI requests.

3. Third-Party Services

Some WTFQ features rely on external services to work. These services may process information according to their own policies.

WTFQ does not control the data-handling practices of third-party providers.

4. External Links

WTFQ contains links to external websites. Once you leave WTFQ, their privacy policies and terms apply.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external websites before using them.

5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WTFQ introduces new features or changes how existing features work. Any updated version will be published on this page.

6. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can visit the WTFQ GitHub repository .
