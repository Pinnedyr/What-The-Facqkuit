Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

At WTFQ, we value your privacy. This page explains what information is handled when you use our website and its tools, including cookies and ads.

1. Who We Are

WTFQ (wtfq.online) is a website with browser games, school tools and links to other websites. WTFQ decides how information on this site is handled. To reach us about privacy, use our contact form.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name or email address to use the site. Searching the game list happens in your browser and is not sent to us.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the visit. These are used to run and secure the site.

3. Cookies and Browser Storage

WTFQ stores small pieces of data in your browser:

  • Ad choice (local storage, key wtfqAds): remembers whether you chose personalized or non-personalized ads. It stays until you clear your browser data.
  • Intro animation (session storage, key wtfqIntro): remembers that you have seen the intro during this visit. It is removed when you close the tab.

These are needed for the site to work the way you chose. Google may also set cookies when ads are shown, as described below.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

WTFQ uses Google AdSense to show ads to all visitors. Ads are how we keep the site free.

By default, ads are non-personalized, which means they are not based on your browsing history. You can choose “Personalized” in our banner to allow personalized ads. Even with non-personalized ads, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, and may process your IP address and device information, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. We do not receive this data about you.

  • You can change your choice at any time with the Ad settings link in the footer of the home page.
  • You can manage personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Third-Party Services Used on Our Pages

  • Google favicon service: the icons on the game tiles are loaded from Google’s favicon service. Your browser requests them from Google, so Google receives your IP address and the domain names of the icons. This happens whatever ad choice you make.
  • Google Forms: our suggestion and contact forms are hosted by Google. What you type there is handled by Google and us under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Other tools: some WTFQ tools, such as the video player or Autofiller (see below), may load content from other services to work.

These services have their own privacy policies, and WTFQ does not control how they handle information.

6. Autofiller

WTFQ Autofiller processes uploaded DOCX documents in your browser. When you choose to use AI autofill, relevant extracted text, such as questions, field labels and document excerpts, is sent to Puter’s AI service to generate suggestions.

This means that document information may be processed by a third-party AI provider. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal or confidential information. Puter’s own privacy policy and terms apply to its processing of AI requests.

7. External Links and Games

The games and tools on WTFQ are run by other websites. When you open a tile, you leave WTFQ and that website’s own privacy policy, cookies, ads and terms apply. We do not control these sites and cannot promise how they handle your information. We recommend reading their policies before using them.

8. Your Rights

If you are in Norway or the European Economic Area, you have rights under data protection law (GDPR), including the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent for personalized ads does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

Because we do not keep accounts or profiles, we hold very little data about you ourselves. To make a request, use our contact form. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Where we rely on legal grounds, personalized ads are based on your consent, and running and securing the site is based on our legitimate interest in keeping it working.

9. Children

WTFQ is made for students and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before you choose personalized ads or use any tool that sends information to another service.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WTFQ introduces new features or changes how existing features work. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

11. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, use our contact form or visit the WTFQ GitHub repository.
